About Me

I am an Ottawa-based multidisciplinary creative working across visual design, audio production, and writing. With a background spanning visual arts, media production, and the humanities, my work explores the intersection of storytelling, culture, identity, and design.

Drawing on my academic background and ongoing studies in interactive media management and design, I combine research-driven storytelling, creative experimentation, and a growing practice in digital design. I am particularly interested in how stories shape our understanding of the world around us, and how different creative mediums can offer new ways of seeing, interpreting, and communicating ideas.

Background

Visual Arts

My creative foundation began in traditional fine arts, with some years of training in realism and pencil drawing. My practice has since expanded into abstract and surrealist approaches, exploring new possibilities through pen, paint, and digital media.

Communications and Collaboration

My experience includes community branding, communications strategy, event coordination, and creative production. I have worked on projects ranging from small-scale community engagement initiatives and fundraising to large-scale events, bringing together reserach, organization, and visual storytelling.

Fashion & Performance

With nearly a decade of experience in fashion modelling, particularly runway, I have also worked extensively behind the scenes in fashion event production, creative direction, and promotion. This has developed my skills for visual commposition, live performance, and collaborative creative work in large teams.
